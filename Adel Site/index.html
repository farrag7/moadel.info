Biography

The Profile

I’m Mohamed Adel, a 23-year-old model and content creator passionate about fashion, lifestyle, and visual storytelling. Born and raised in Egypt, I have always been deeply passionate about fashion and modeling, collaborating with esteemed brands across the region.

أنا محمد عادل سني ٢٣ سنة بحب مجال الفاشون والفيديوهات. أعمل مع العلامات التجارية لإنشاء محتوى أصيل وعصري وجذاب بصريًا يربط بين العلامة التجارية وجمهورها.

Beyond fashion editorial work, I have starred in several high-profile ad campaigns for hotels and luxury hospitality brands in Egypt and Dubai.

A flexible, down-to-earth, and charismatic individual, I am an athletic person who enjoys a morning run, traveling, and free diving.
